## The brief

> Write a blind review of a classmate's one-page proposal that you would be
> willing to sign, and then don't.

Proposals are submitted anonymously and redistributed anonymously; you
won't know whose work you're reviewing, and you won't know who reviewed
yours until marks are returned. The point isn't the disguise --- it's
whether removing the social cost of a specific claim changes what you're
willing to write, and whether you can tell the difference between using
that freedom honestly and using it as cover.

## What you submit

A written review of the proposal you're assigned, following the same
specific-and-falsifiable standard as every other week. No names, no
identifying references to who you think the author is, even if you're
confident.
