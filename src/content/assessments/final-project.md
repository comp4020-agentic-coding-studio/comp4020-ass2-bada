## The brief

> Prove, with evidence rather than assertion, whether one method for giving
> feedback actually survives six unrelated domains.

By week 11 you'll have written notes on visual work, code, prose, a blind
proposal, a published-style review, and a brief. The Final Project collects
the strongest of each into a portfolio, and asks you to argue --- using the
notes themselves as evidence --- whether "specific, falsifiable, about the
work" is a real method or a description that happened to fit this
semester's assignments.

The scope that fits twelve weeks is six short notes and a two-page
argument, not a rewrite of any of them into something longer. The work was
already done, week by week; this is the case for what it adds up to.

## What you submit

One document: the six notes, each with
the piece of work it was about named or linked, and a two-page argument
that uses the notes as evidence for or against the semester's claim ---
including, explicitly, your weakest note and what it shows.
