Marisol wrote restaurant and gallery reviews for a living before she
convened this course, which is why the theory weeks keep returning to
criticism as a published genre rather than to lecture-hall abstractions. She
teaches every lecture and leads the crits on criticism-as-genre, receiving
notes, and the portfolio.

Her one rule, repeated often enough that students start saying it back to
her: a note that doesn't name a specific line, frame or function isn't a
note, it's a mood.
