## Bring

A small pull request or patch of your own --- ten to eighty lines is the
sweet spot, big enough to have a real decision in it, small enough to review
in the room.

## The crit

Same silent-note-first structure as week 3, aimed at a diff instead of a
drawing. "This could be cleaner" doesn't survive here any better than it did
on the drawing --- the room's standard question becomes "cleaner how, and
what would convince you it already is." Idris runs this one hardest, because
he's heard every version of a vague code review that still got approved.

## Leaves with

A review comment on a real diff that names the line and the failing case,
not the vibe.
