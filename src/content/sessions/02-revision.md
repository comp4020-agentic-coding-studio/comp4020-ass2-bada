## Bring

The note from week 1 that didn't work --- the vague one --- and, if you can
still get at it, the piece of work it was about.

## The crit

Everyone rewrites their own vague note before the room sees it: same
complaint, same piece of work, but naming the specific thing and saying what
would have to change to prove the new version wrong. Then we swap, and the
room tries to break the rewrite --- if a partner can't find the specific
thing you claimed to name, the rewrite hasn't earned the pass yet.

Unlike every week from here to week 9, this one doesn't introduce a new
critique domain. It's here on purpose, straight after orientation: before
the method has to survive a domain change, it has to survive being pointed
back at something you already have feelings about.

## Leaves with

A before/after pair --- the vague note and your rewrite --- that you'll want
again for the portfolio in week 11.
