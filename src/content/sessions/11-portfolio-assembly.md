## Bring

Every note you've written this semester --- visual art, code, prose,
peer review, review-as-genre, the brief itself --- laid out, not tidied up.

## The crit

You read your own semester back to the room in order, out loud, and the room
grades your weakest note against your strongest using nothing but this
week's criteria: specific, falsifiable, about the work. Most people find
their weakest note is from a domain they were least comfortable in, which is
itself the finding --- the method holds up worst exactly where you trusted
your own taste most and checked it least.

## Leaves with

A shortlist for the Final Project portfolio, and an honest answer to the
question from week 1: does your definition of a note still hold, eleven
weeks and six domains later?
