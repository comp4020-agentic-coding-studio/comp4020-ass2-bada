## Bring

A short published review of your choosing --- restaurant, film, book,
anything --- that you think is actually good, and a first draft of your own
review of something you experienced this week.

## The crit

We take apart the published review first: where's the specific claim, where
does the writer tell you what would change their mind, where does it slide
into pure verdict with nothing under it. Then the same pass on your own
drafts. This is the domain where "care" gets tested hardest in the other
direction from prose --- the audience isn't the person who made the thing,
so the temptation is to stop being specific and just perform an opinion.

## Leaves with

A published-style review with at least one line a stranger could disagree
with on the evidence, not just on taste.
