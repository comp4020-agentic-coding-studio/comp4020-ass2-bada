## Bring

A piece of visual work you've made --- a drawing, a photo, a layout,
whatever you've got --- and, separately, one note ready to give on someone
else's.

## The crit

Work goes up one at a time. Before anyone speaks, the room has sixty seconds
to write a note in silence: what specific choice in the piece the note is
about, and what would have to be true of the piece for the note to be
wrong. Notes get read aloud after, not before, so the first reaction in the
room isn't whoever spoke fastest.

## Leaves with

One note given and at least one received that both pass this week's lecture
test --- specific enough to point at, falsifiable enough to argue with.
