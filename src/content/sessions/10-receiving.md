## Bring

Work that's already been through a crit this semester, and the notes it got.

## The crit

One person's work, one note at a time, live: the note gets read, and before
anything else the person it's aimed at has to repeat it back accurately ---
not agree with it, just prove they heard the specific claim, not a
generalised sting. Only after that can they respond. Most of what goes wrong
in receiving a note happens in the gap between hearing it and repeating it,
which this format makes impossible to skip.

## Leaves with

Practice separating "I heard the specific claim" from "I agree with it,"
which turns out to be most of what makes a room safe to give real notes in.
