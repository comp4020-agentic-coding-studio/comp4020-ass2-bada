## Bring

A brief you've been given this semester --- an assessment page, a session
spec, a marking criterion --- and a note on it, written as if it were a
piece of work someone was asking you to review.

## The crit

The room picks apart a handful of briefs from this course, this semester,
this week's Peer Review Exchange included, on the same terms as every piece
of work before it: what's specific, what's vague, what would prove a
complaint about it wrong. A brief that can't survive its own method isn't
one this course gets to keep without saying so.

## Leaves with

A note on a real brief that names the specific clause doing the vague work
--- the same shape of finding as every other week, aimed one level up.
