## Bring

Two pieces of feedback you've been given, on anything, ever: the one that
actually changed what you did next, and the one that didn't. Not the meanest
one and the nicest one --- the one that worked and the one that didn't.

## The crit

We read a handful of these out loud, anonymised, and sort them into two
piles before anyone says why. The pile that worked turns out to share three
things, most weeks without much prompting: it names something specific, it
says what would have to be different to be wrong, and it stays on the work.
The pile that didn't share the opposite three. That's the whole method for
the semester --- everything after this week is that claim tested against a
new kind of work.

## Leaves with

A one-sentence definition of a note, written in your own words, that you'll
be asked to defend again in week 11 once you've tested it eleven times.
