## Bring

A page of your own prose --- an essay draft, a story, an email you're not
sure about --- printed or on a shared doc the room can mark up directly.

## The crit

Line edits, in pairs: mark the page sentence by sentence, then talk through
only the marks, not the page as a whole. This is the week this week's
lecture is really for --- prose is where "the work, not the person" gets
hardest to keep straight, because a sentence sounds like a person talking.
The rule holds anyway: a mark on a sentence, defensible on that sentence
alone.

## Leaves with

A marked-up page with at least three edits you could each defend to the
person who wrote them, out loud, without it turning into a comment about the
person.
