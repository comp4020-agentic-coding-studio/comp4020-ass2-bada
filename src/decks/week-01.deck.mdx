# The Devastating Note

Week 1 --- orientation

---

## Most feedback isn't a note

It's a reaction, dressed as an assessment.

- no specific location
- no way to be proven wrong
- often aimed at the person, not the work

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# A note is specific, falsifiable, and about the work

---

## Specific

Points at a location: a sentence, a line, a region of a canvas.

"The pacing drags" --- not specific.

"The scene on page four repeats what the reader already knows" --- specific.

---

## Falsifiable

Says what would prove it wrong.

A note you can't lose an argument about was never really a claim.

---

## About the work

The tell is the noun.

A note about a sentence names the sentence.
A note about a person names a trait.

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## Six domains, one method

visual art · code · prose · peer review · criticism · the brief itself

If it only survives one, it isn't a method.
